Dutchman's Pipe Club

An invitation-only private golf and racquets club at 1900 Banyan Club Road, West Palm Beach, Florida 33401. Telephone +1 561 557 5840.

Golf

A Jack Nicklaus Signature championship course measuring 7,300 yards from the tips with a 75.8 course rating, played without tee times. Practice facilities include a 315-yard driving range with four target greens, a 12,000 square foot putting green and a two-acre short game complex. Every round is accompanied by a professional caddie, and club fitting is brand agnostic and data driven.

Instruction

Coaching led by nationally recognised professionals, including GOLF Magazine Top 100 Teachers and Golf Digest Top 50 Instructors, with a future Golf Performance Center planned.

Racquet Sports

Tennis, padel and pickleball, with clinics, leagues and social play. The club runs one of the area's few private padel programs.

Location

East of I-95 in West Palm Beach, minutes from Palm Beach Island, Worth Avenue, downtown West Palm Beach and President Donald J. Trump International Airport.

Membership

Membership is by private introduction. Categories are Full Golf, Next Gen. for members under 40, Visiting for seasonal residents, Corporate and Social. Opportunities are limited. For enquiries, email Membership@dutchmanspipeclub.com.
